# FooteYoder1997BigBallOfMud

## Summary

- Describes how systems drift into unmanaged, hard-to-reason structures when architecture discipline weakens.
- Shows that complexity can accumulate incrementally through local expedient choices.
- Explains why architectural debt is usually organizational and process-shaped, not just code-shaped.
- Emphasizes continuous refactoring and boundary discipline as survival mechanisms.

## Relevance To Hephaestus

- Helps guard against collapsing policy, runtime, and UI responsibilities into one mutable core.
- Supports keeping domain logic explicit and isolated through blueprint phases.
- Reinforces why growth in autonomy must be matched by structural decomposition.

## Hephaestus Would Be Worse Without It

Hephaestus would be more likely to re-centralize complexity and lose architectural clarity as features accumulate.
