## Summary

- Provides a systems-theoretic hazard analysis method centered on control structures and constraints.
- Emphasizes unsafe control actions and missing feedback loops rather than component failure alone.
- Offers practical procedures for deriving safety constraints from operational scenarios.
- Supports integrating safety analysis into architecture and operations, not only post-incident review.

## Relevance To Hephaestus

- Useful for analyzing autopilot boundaries, approval controls, and operator intervention channels.
- Supports D3-D6 safety design across runtime policy and control plane behavior.
- Helps convert abstract risk concerns into actionable control constraints.

## Hephaestus Would Be Worse Without It

Hephaestus would have weaker methods for identifying unsafe control interactions in autonomy and oversight workflows.
