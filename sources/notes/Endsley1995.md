# Endsley1995

Citation: Endsley, M. R. (1995). Toward a Theory of Situation Awareness in Dynamic Systems. Human Factors, 37(1), 32-64.

Public URL or DOI: https://doi.org/10.1518/001872095779049543
Local Copy: not present
Status: note
Blueprint Phases: D6

## Summary

- Breaks situation awareness into perception, comprehension, and projection.
- Explains why operators fail when systems hide salient state or future consequences.
- Connects interface design directly to decision quality under time pressure.
- Makes awareness a system property, not just an operator skill issue.

## Relevance To Hephaestus

- The control plane needs to show current state, meaning, and likely next outcomes.
- Approval queues should surface why a decision is needed and what happens if the operator waits.
- D6 cockpit design should be evaluated against operator awareness, not just aesthetics.

## Hephaestus Would Be Worse Without It

Hephaestus could present raw state without enough meaning for an operator to act correctly under pressure.
