Hephaestus Upgrade Telemetry Paper
Date: 2026-05-29

Objective
Before executing the seeded 120-ticket roadmap, establish a telemetry baseline and control-loop metrics so queue behavior is measurable and optimization decisions are evidence-driven.

Primary Telemetry Questions
1. Is queue pressure rising faster than execution throughput?
2. Are pending tickets aging beyond acceptable execution windows?
3. Is superseded churn indicating over-fragmented intake?
4. Are allowlist/policy denials a dominant failure mechanism?
5. Is the system producing completions in the most recent 24h window?

Telemetry Metrics to Observe
Queue State
- total tickets
- pending tickets
- in-progress tickets
- awaiting-approval tickets
- blocked/failed/stale tickets
- queue pressure index

Aging
- pending age p50 (hours)
- pending age p95 (hours)
- pending age max (hours)

Execution
- total attempts
- attempts per ticket
- retry rate
- started in last 24h
- completed in last 24h
- admission-to-start latency p95 (ms)

Churn
- superseded rate among terminal outcomes
- cancelled rate among terminal outcomes
- superseded-to-completed ratio

Policy and Failure Taxonomy
- allowlist denial count
- allowlist denial rate
- top failure buckets by frequency

Observation Targets
- superseded rate <= 0.18
- allowlist denial rate <= 0.08
- pending age p95 <= 36h
- queue pressure index <= 0.65
- completed last 24h >= 1

Alert Conditions
- superseded-rate-high
- allowlist-denial-rate-high
- pending-age-p95-high
- queue-pressure-high
- no-completions-last-24h

Planned Telemetry Workflow
1. Capture baseline telemetry snapshot before queue execution.
2. Execute queue wave activity.
3. Capture post-execution telemetry snapshot.
4. Compare alert set, queue pressure, and churn metrics.
5. Feed results into next ticket-wave shaping and policy updates.

Expected Value
- More reliable differentiation between productive throughput and churn.
- Better signal for whether tandem mode or autonomous mode is the limiting factor.
- Faster policy iteration by ranking denial/failure buckets with measured impact.
